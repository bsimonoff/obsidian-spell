## What They're Looking For

Just-Evotec Biologics manufactures novel biotherapeutics (monoclonal antibodies, cell therapies, gene therapies, etc.) for pharmaceutical clients. Process Engineers execute upstream and/or downstream manufacturing operations.

### Responsibilities (Process Engineer I)
- Execute biopharmaceutical manufacturing processes (cell culture, purification, fill-finish)
- Monitor and record process parameters (temperature, pH, flow rates, etc.)
- Troubleshoot manufacturing issues; document root causes
- Support process development and scale-up activities
- Collaborate with scientists, operations, and quality teams
- Contribute to continuous improvement projects

### Requirements
- **Bachelor's degree** in:
  - **Chemical Engineering** (preferred for ChemE grads)
  - Biological sciences, chemistry, biochemistry
  - Engineering or related life science field
- 0–2 years of relevant experience or recent graduate
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Ability to work in a fast-paced biotech environment
- Teamwork and communication
