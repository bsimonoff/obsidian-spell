**"Describe your understanding of bioreactor control."**
> "Bioreactors require precise control of multiple interacting variables: dissolved oxygen, pH, temperature, glucose delivery, nutrient feeding based on cell culture kinetics. These parameters drive cell growth and productivity. Maintaining setpoints and responding to disturbances requires real-time monitoring and automated feedback—which is exactly what DeltaV enables."

I'd check calibration and sensor integrity, review documentation and SOPs, consult with team members, and escalate if needed. The key is systematic approach and documentation."

**"Give an example of working in a compliance-heavy environment."**
