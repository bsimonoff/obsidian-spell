### Automation Technology at Pfizer

**DeltaV (Emerson) Deployment:**
- 150+ bioreactors across Pfizer sites running DeltaV
- **BioNet DeltaV system** = proprietary advanced process control platform built on top of standard DeltaV
- **Capabilities**:
  - Automated glucose feedback control
  - pH standardization across bioreactors
  - Variable-speed pump control
  - Zone management (temperature, DO)
  - Python-based control functions deployable within 2 hours
- **System health monitoring** continuous at some facilities (e.g., Sanford, NC)
- Highly integrated, modern automation infrastructure

**Other Systems**:
- Allen-Bradley PLC systems
- OSI PI data infrastructure (historian, analytics)
- Ethernet, DH+, ControlNet networking

**Implication for the role**: You'll be supporting and troubleshooting one of Pfizer's most sophisticated automation environments. Ramp-up opportunity is high; exposure to cutting-edge control systems is guaranteed.

## Why This Role Matters (Strategic Context)

### Biopharmaceutical Manufacturing Automation Trends

1. **Regulatory Shift**: EMA's new Annex 22 (AI in GMP, July 2025) signals regulatory embrace of advanced automation and data analytics. This accelerates adoption of sophisticated automation systems like DeltaV.

2. **Compliance as Competitive Advantage**: 78% of pharma companies now use automation for compliance management. Robust automation = faster regulatory approvals, fewer inspection findings.

3. **Supply Chain Resilience**: Post-COVID, manufacturing flexibility is critical. Single-use bioreactor automation enables rapid changeovers and scale-up—a strategic capability Pfizer is investing in (Andover facility).

4. **Career Trajectory**: Process automation engineers are in high demand. The role is entry-level but has clear growth to senior technical specialist or operations leadership.

## Interview Prep (Likely Questions)

- "Tell me about your experience with DCS systems." → *Honest answer: "I'm new to formal DCS, but my ChemE background gave me the process engineering foundation to understand control logic, instrumentation, and system interactions. I'm eager to master DeltaV through certification and on-the-job training."*
- "Why are you interested in 2nd shift?" → *Be genuine. Flexibility? Preference for quieter shift? Opportunity for hands-on work? Whatever is true.*
- "What's your understanding of bioreactor control?" → *Show you understand the underlying process: cell culture kinetics, DO, pH, temperature control, nutrient delivery, etc.*
- "How would you approach troubleshooting an automation failure?" → *Systematic: understand the process, check sensors, review DCS logs, consult documentation, escalate if needed.*
- "Describe your experience with compliance and documentation." → *Lab work, SOP adherence, precise data entry, etc.*
