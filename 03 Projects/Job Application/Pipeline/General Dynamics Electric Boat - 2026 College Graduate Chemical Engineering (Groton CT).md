## What They're Looking For

Chemical engineers at Electric Boat work on designs for the next class of submarine (Virginia and Columbia class), troubleshoot deckplate issues with building/testing submarines, participate in design reviews, and grow alongside experienced mentors.

### Key Responsibilities
- Participate in submarine design and engineering reviews
- Support manufacturing and construction of Virginia and Columbia-class submarines
- Troubleshoot production/assembly issues on the deckplate
- Apply chemical engineering principles to marine propulsion, materials, and systems
- Collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams (materials, mechanical, electrical, controls)

### Requirements
- **Bachelor of Science in Chemical Engineering** (graduation 2026)
- U.S. citizenship or ability to obtain security clearance (defense contractor  typical for this role)
- Strong problem-solving and communication skills
- Familiarity with design review processes, P&ID reading, or process simulation is a plus
