## What They're Looking For

Cleveland-Cliffs is the leading flat-rolled steel producer in North America. They're seeking recent college graduates for structured entry-level roles in process engineering, quality control, and manufacturing optimization.

### Typical Responsibilities (for Associate Chemical Engineer)
- Support process control and optimization on steelmaking furnaces
- Analyze and interpret quality data (chemistry, mechanical properties)
- Collaborate with operations to troubleshoot production issues
- Apply chemical principles to ore processing, casting, and rolling
- Contribute to continuous improvement projects
- May rotate through different mill areas (blast furnace, basic oxygen furnace, continuous casting, rolling)
- Support environmental and emissions compliance monitoring

### Requirements (typical for Cleveland-Cliffs entry roles)
- **Bachelor of Science in Chemical Engineering** (or related field: Materials, Mechanical, Environmental)
- Recent graduate or graduating 2026
- Strong fundamentals in thermodynamics, unit operations, process control
- Interest in manufacturing and heavy industry
- Analytical and problem-solving mindset
- Teamwork and communication skills

   - Proximity to Chicago (day trip or relocation option)
   - Access to Lake Michigan / outdoor recreation
 **Sector risk:** Steel industry is cyclical; downturns can affect hiring/promotion. However, flat-rolled steel for autos/appliances is relatively stable.
