## What They're Looking For

DuPont's entry-level rotational program is designed to accelerate new engineers. Rotations expose you to manufacturing, process engineering, quality, R&D, and operations across Qnity Electronics' operations (advanced materials for semiconductors, packaging, displays, etc.).

### Typical Rotation Areas (for ChemE)
1. **Process Engineering:** Optimize manufacturing processes for electronics materials (thin films, coatings, precision materials)
2. **Manufacturing Engineering:** Support production lines, troubleshoot yield issues, implement improvements
3. **Quality Engineering:** Define specifications, statistical process control, failure analysis
4. **Operations/Supply Chain:** Materials planning, capacity optimization
5. **R&D Support:** Collaborate on new material development or process innovations (less common for entry rotations, but possible)

### Requirements
- **Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or Electrical Engineering** (graduating 2026)
- Strong academic foundation (GPA 3.0+, though can negotiate)
- Analytical and problem-solving mindset
- Willingness to relocate to Wilmington, DE
- Interest in manufacturing and continuous improvement
- Teamwork and communication skills
