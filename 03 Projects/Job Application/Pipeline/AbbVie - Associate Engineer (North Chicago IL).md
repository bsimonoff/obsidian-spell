## What They're Looking For

AbbVie manufactures and develops pharmaceuticals across immunology, oncology, virology, and neuroscience. Associate Engineers support manufacturing, process development, quality, or operations.

### Associate Engineer Responsibilities
- Support pharmaceutical manufacturing operations and process development
- Monitor and troubleshoot manufacturing processes
- Conduct lab-scale or pilot-scale experiments
- Analyze data; document findings and improvements
- Collaborate with operations, quality, R&D, and supply chain
- Contribute to continuous improvement and cost reduction
- Support regulatory compliance and GMP

### Requirements
- **Bachelor's degree** in:
  - **Chemical Engineering** (ideal fit)
  - Mechanical, Civil, Environmental, or other engineering discipline
  - Chemistry, biology, or life science
- 02 years of relevant experience or internships
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Teamwork and communication
