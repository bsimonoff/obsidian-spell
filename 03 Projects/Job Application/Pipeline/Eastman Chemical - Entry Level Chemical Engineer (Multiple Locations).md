## What They're Looking For

Eastman is a global specialty chemical company producing advanced materials for consumer, industrial, and specialty markets (coatings, adhesives, sealants, engineered materials, fibers, etc.). Entry-level roles typically support process engineering, manufacturing, quality, or plant operations.

### Typical Responsibilities (Process/Manufacturing Engineer I)
- Operate or support chemical manufacturing processes (reactors, distillation, crystallization, etc.)
- Monitor and record process parameters (temperature, pressure, flow, composition)
- Troubleshoot production issues; identify and implement improvements
- Collaborate with operations, quality, and engineering teams
- Contribute to continuous improvement projects (Lean, Six Sigma initiatives)
- Support maintenance and capital projects
- Apply chemistry and engineering fundamentals to real-world manufacturing

### Requirements
- **Bachelor of Science in Chemical Engineering** (graduating or recent graduate 2026)
- Strong fundamentals in thermodynamics, unit operations, process control, material science
- Ability to work in a fast-paced manufacturing environment
- Problem-solving and analytical mindset
- Teamwork and communication
- Willingness to work rotating shifts (some facilities require 24/7 coverage)

## Potential Concerns

 **Shift work:** Many Eastman facilities operate 24/7; entry-level roles may require rotating shifts (8-hour, 12-hour, or weird schedules). Verify schedule in interviews.  
 **Location:** Most sites are in smaller towns (Kingsport TN, Longview TX, Beaumont TX, Charleston SC). Not ideal if you want major metro vibe. However:
   - Kingsport has growing tech/professional community
   - Charleston is a beautiful, growing city
   - Texas locations near Houston (2+ hrs)
   - Cost of living is much lower than Denver/Seattle/NYC
 **Chemical manufacturing environment:** You'll be in plants with heat, noise, industrial hazards. PPE required. Not for everyone.  
 **Broad salary range:** Glassdoor shows $67130k, but entry-level is lower end ($6780k). Don't assume upper range.  
 **Industry maturity:** Specialty chemicals is mature (not as fast-growing as tech/biotech); career growth is steady but not explosive.
