## What They're Looking For

Dennis Group is an engineering firm specializing in design, construction, and commissioning of food and beverage processing facilities. Process Engineers develop facility layouts, P&IDs, equipment specifications, and manage construction/startup phases.

### Key Responsibilities
- Design process flow diagrams (PFDs) and piping & instrumentation diagrams (P&IDs)
- Size and specify process equipment (heat exchangers, mixing tanks, pumps, etc.)
- Develop project scopes, budgets, and work schedules
- Support field installation teams and construction site observations
- Collaborate with mechanical, electrical, and controls engineers
- Provide expertise in controls, packaging, mechanical design during construction
- Commission and start-up new facilities

### Requirements
- **Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering OR Process Engineering** (2026 graduate acceptable)
- 03 years of work experience OR relevant coursework in:
  - Process design & simulation
  - P&ID development
  - Equipment sizing and specification
  - Project management basics
- **Required software:** MS Office, MS Project, AutoCAD (or willingness to learn)
- **Travel:** 3050% of year (on-site construction project management)
- Strong communication and problem-solving skills
- Interest in food safety standards is a plus
