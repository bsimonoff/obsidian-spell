## About the Role

Vertex Pharmaceuticals is a leader in cell and genetic therapies. The Lab Automation Engineer will join a team designing, implementing, and validating automation solutions across multiple platforms (Hamilton, Biomek, etc.). This role supports multiple disease areas and advances automation capabilities while developing understanding of R&D processes.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Translate manual R&D processes into automated system designs
- Troubleshoot automation system problems and document solutions
- Support commissioning, validation, and daily operations in cross-functional teams
- Identify continuous improvement opportunities and drive implementation
- Design automation for both continuous and batch processes
- Provide technical guidance on automation system operations
- Participate in advancement of current and new automation capabilities
- Comply with safety and GMP procedures

## Requirements

**Required:**
- Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering, Computer Sciences, or related field
