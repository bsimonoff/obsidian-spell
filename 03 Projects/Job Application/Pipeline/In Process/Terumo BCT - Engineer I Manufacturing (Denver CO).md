## What They're Looking For

Terumo BCT manufactures and processes medical devices for blood/cell collection, separation, manufacturing, and therapeutic applications. Engineer I roles support manufacturing, process improvement, quality, or equipment engineering.

### Typical Responsibilities (Manufacturing/Process Engineer I)
- Support manufacturing operations (equipment operation, troubleshooting, maintenance)
- Analyze process parameters (yield, quality, efficiency)
- Troubleshoot manufacturing issues
- Support process validation and equipment qualification
- Collaborate with operations, quality, engineering, and R&D
- Contribute to continuous improvement (Lean/Six Sigma)
- Document processes and maintain regulatory compliance

### Requirements
- **Bachelor's degree** in Chemical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or related field
- 02 years of experience (recent graduates welcome)
- Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
- Manufacturing or process industry experience is a plus
- Familiarity with GxP (GMP, SOP) concepts is valuable but not required for entry-level
