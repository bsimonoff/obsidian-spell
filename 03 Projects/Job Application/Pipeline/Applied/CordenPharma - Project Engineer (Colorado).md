## About the Role

CordenPharma is a leading CDMO specializing in active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), excipients, drug products, and packaging. The Project Engineer manages capital projects supporting pharmaceutical drug substance, intermediates, and fine chemical development and production across six technology platforms: Peptides, Lipids & Carbohydrates, Injectables, Highly Potent & Oncology, Small Molecules, and Oligonucleotides.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Manage and execute high-value capital projects across diverse disciplines: civil, mechanical, equipment design, facilities, technology transfer, and product delivery
- Develop project business case, objectives, and evaluate alternatives with stakeholder consensus
- Plan, coordinate, and oversee engineering efforts while tracking cost, schedule, and quality
- Review designs for compliance with engineering principles, company standards, and regulatory requirements
- Oversee project qualifications and performance delivery
- Support user requirements specifications, scope development, process design concepts, funding requests, equipment specifications, change control, and validation efforts
- Provide technical leadership to contract engineers and project teams
