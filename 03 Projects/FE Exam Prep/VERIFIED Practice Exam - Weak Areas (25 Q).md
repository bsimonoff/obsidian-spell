C) 1.5 M

A) 2.2 g

## ANSWER KEY (WITH FULL JUSTIFICATION)

**Q1: B) 1.0 M**
- Moles of NaCl = 5.85 g / 58.5 g/mol = 0.1 mol
- Molarity = 0.1 mol / 0.1 L = 1.0 M ✓

**Q2: B) 4.4 g**
- Moles of CaCO₃ = 10 g / 100 g/mol = 0.1 mol
- From stoichiometry: 1 mol CaCO₃ → 1 mol CO₂
- Moles of CO₂ = 0.1 mol
- Mass of CO₂ = 0.1 mol × 44 g/mol = 4.4 g ✓

**Q3: B) 4.74**
- pKa = –log(1.8 × 10⁻⁵) = 4.74
- pH = 4.74 + log(0.1/0.1) = 4.74 + log(1) = 4.74 + 0 = 4.74 ✓

**Q4: C) +0.78 V**
- Cathode (reduction): Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu, E° = +0.34 V
- Anode (oxidation): Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻, E° = –(–0.44) = +0.44 V
- E°_cell = E°_cathode – E°_anode = 0.34 – (–0.44) = 0.78 V ✓

**Q5: B) 18**
- For CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ with n = 8: H = 2(8) + 2 = 18 ✓

**Q6: C) 0.10 mol**
- Moles of H₂SO₄ = 0.025 L × 2 M = 0.05 mol
- Stoichiometry: 1 mol H₂SO₄ requires 2 mol NaOH
- Moles of NaOH = 0.05 × 2 = 0.10 mol ✓

**Q7: A) 0.001 M**
- Moles of protein = 6 g / 60,000 g/mol = 0.0001 mol = 1 × 10⁻⁴ mol
- Molarity = 1 × 10⁻⁴ mol / 0.1 L = 1 × 10⁻³ M = 0.001 M ✓

**Q8: B) 2**
- Moles of glucose = 180 g / 180 g/mol = 1 mol
- From stoichiometry: 1 mol glucose → 2 mol ethanol
- Moles of ethanol = 2 mol ✓

**Q9: B) 6**
- Mean = (2+4+6+8+10) / 5 = 30 / 5 = 6 ✓

**Q10: B) 2.83**
- Mean = 6
- Deviations: (–4)², (–2)², 0², 2², 4² = 16, 4, 0, 4, 16
- Variance = (16+4+0+4+16) / 5 = 40/5 = 8
- Std Dev = √8 = 2.828 ≈ 2.83 ✓

**Q11: B) 100**
- Expected defects = 5,000 × 0.02 = 100 ✓

**Q12: B) 0.375**
- P(X=2) = C(4,2) × (0.5)² × (0.5)²
- C(4,2) = 6
- P = 6 × 0.25 × 0.25 = 6 × 0.0625 = 0.375 ✓

**Q13: A) 0.18**
- P(A AND B) = 0.3 × 0.6 = 0.18 ✓

**Q14: C) 2.0**
- z = (70 – 50) / 10 = 20/10 = 2.0 ✓

**Q15: C) 2.00**
- Data: 98, 100, 102
- Mean = (98+100+102) / 3 = 100
- Deviations: –2, 0, +2
- Sum of squared deviations = 4 + 0 + 4 = 8
- Sample variance (using n–1) = 8 / 2 = 4
- Sample std dev = √4 = 2.00 ✓

**Q16: B) 200 MPa**
- Allowable stress = Yield / Safety factor = 400 / 2.0 = 200 MPa ✓

**Q17: A) 0.98 g**
- Volume = (5 mm)³ = 125 mm³ = 125 × 10⁻⁹ m³
- Mass = 7850 kg/m³ × 125 × 10⁻⁹ m³ = 0.98125 × 10⁻³ kg = 0.98 g ✓

**Q18: C) 4**
- FCC has 4 atoms per unit cell (8 corners × 1/8 + 6 faces × 1/2) ✓

**Q19: D) 500 MPa**
- Stress = Force / Area = 50,000 N / (100 × 10⁻⁶ m²) = 50 × 10⁶ Pa = 500 MPa ✓

**Q20: A) Mean = 150, Amplitude = 150**
- Mean stress = (300 + 0) / 2 = 150 MPa
- Stress amplitude = (300 – 0) / 2 = 150 MPa ✓

**Q21: B) 1.98 g/cm³**
- ρ = 0.60 × 2.5 + 0.40 × 1.2 = 1.5 + 0.48 = 1.98 g/cm³ ✓

**Q22: C) 0.73 M**
- [A] = [A]₀ × e^(–kt) = 2.0 × e^(–0.1×10) = 2.0 × e^(–1.0) = 2.0 × 0.3679 = 0.7358 ≈ 0.73 M ✓

**Q23: A) 7.36 kW**
- P = 1000 × 9.81 × 15 × 0.05 = 7,357.5 W ≈ 7.36 kW ✓

**Q24: B) 0.50 M**
- Residence time τ = V / Q = 250 L / 50 L/min = 5 min
- C_out = 1.0 / (1 + 0.2 × 5) = 1.0 / (1 + 1.0) = 1.0 / 2.0 = 0.50 M ✓

**Q25: C) 4**
- Rate = k[A][B]
- If [A] doubles and [B] doubles: Rate_new = k × 2[A] × 2[B] = 4 × k[A][B]
- Rate increases by factor of 4 ✓
