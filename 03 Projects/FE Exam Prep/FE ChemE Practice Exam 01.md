# Part 2 — NCEES-Style Question Formats (Q21–Q27)

*These mirror the non-standard formats on the real NCEES/Pearson exam: matching ("drag the correct… to each…"), multi-select ("select the two/three that apply"), figure-described, and the ethics/safety/professional-practice topics that always appear.*

**Q21. (Matching)** Drag each separation process to the physical property it primarily exploits.
*Refer to FE Handbook p. 243 (Chemical Engineering — Mass Transfer/Separations).*
- Distillation → **difference in relative volatility (boiling point)**
- Liquid-liquid extraction → **difference in solubility (distribution coefficient)**
- Membrane/ultra-filtration → **difference in molecule/particle size**
- Adsorption → **difference in surface affinity**

**Q22. (Select the THREE that apply)** Which of the following are **intensive** properties of a system?
*Refer to FE Handbook p. 143 (Thermodynamics) for intensive vs. extensive properties.*
- A) Temperature      intensive
- B) Mass
- C) Density              intensive
- D) Volume
- E) Pressure              intensive

**Q23. (Select the TWO that apply)** Which of the following devices are used to protect a pressure vessel from an overpressure condition?
*Refer to FE Handbook p. 243 (Chemical Engineering — Safety) and process safety references.*
- A) Rupture disc                    this
- B) Spring-loaded relief valve                this
- C) Orifice plate flowmeter
- D) Rotameter
- E) Thermocouple

**Q24. (Figure-described)** The set point of a temperature controller on a reactor is stepped up at $t=0$. The recorded response **overshoots** the new set point and then **oscillates with steadily decaying amplitude** before settling at the set point. This closed-loop response is best described as:
*Refer to FE Handbook p. 243 (Chemical Engineering — Process Control) for second-order system response.*
- A) Overdamped
- B) Critically damped
- C) Underdamped                this
- D) Unstable

**Q25. (Ethics)** According to the fundamental canons of engineering codes of ethics (e.g., NSPE), an engineer's **paramount** obligation is to:
*Refer to FE Handbook (Ethics & Professional Practice).*
- A) The client who pays for the work
- B) The employer's financial interests
- C) The public health, safety, and welfare             this
- D) The reputation of the engineering profession

**Q26. (Safety — chemical compatibility)** A facility worker intends to combine two liquids in the same drum. Which combination poses the greatest **acute inhalation hazard** due to the evolution of a toxic gas?
*Refer to FE Handbook (Safety, Health & Environment) and chemical-compatibility principles.*
- A) Sodium hypochlorite (bleach) + hydrochloric acid                   this
- B) Sodium chloride + water
- C) Ethanol + water
- D) Sodium bicarbonate + water

**Q27. (Matching)** Drag each U.S. regulatory agency to its primary function.
*Refer to FE Handbook (Safety, Health & Environment — regulatory agencies).*
- Regulates worker safety and health → **OSHA**
- Regulates environmental protection → **EPA**
- Regulates handling of radioactive materials → **NRC**
- Regulates food and pharmaceutical safety → **FDA**
