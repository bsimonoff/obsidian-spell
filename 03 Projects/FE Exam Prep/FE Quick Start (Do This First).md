## Step 4: Assess Your Baseline (1-2 hours)

### Take Diagnostic Exam
1. Find a free practice exam or use first half of NCEES handbook examples
2. Set timer: 5 hours 20 minutes (full FE duration)
3. Take the exam in ONE sitting under test conditions:
   - Quiet room
   - No phone, no internet (except calculator)
   - Use only NCEES handbook as reference
   - Pencil and paper only (no computer)
4. Score yourself
5. **Document results:**
   - Total score: ___/110
   - Percentage: ____%
   - Questions you got wrong (note topics)

### Analyze Weak Areas
Create a note: `03 Projects/FE Exam Prep/Study Notes/Diagnostic_Exam_Analysis.md`

Write:
- Which topics were hardest?
- Did you struggle with time management?
- Which areas need the most review?
- Any topics you felt confident about?

**Example:**
```
DIAGNOSTIC EXAM ANALYSIS
Date: July 6, 2026
Score: 58/110 (53%) — Not passing yet; OK for baseline

- Thermodynamics: 4/11 (36%) ← FOCUS HERE

- Process Control: 1/4 (25%) ← FOCUS HERE

- Math basics: 8/10 (80%) ✓
```
