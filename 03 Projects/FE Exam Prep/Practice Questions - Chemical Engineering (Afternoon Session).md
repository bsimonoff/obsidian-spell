## THERMODYNAMICS & ENERGY BALANCES (Questions 1–11)

**1.** A steady-flow process involves water flowing into a tank at 50°C with an enthalpy of 209 kJ/kg. The water exits at 80°C with an enthalpy of 335 kJ/kg. The mass flow rate is 2 kg/s. Assuming kinetic and potential energy changes are negligible and no shaft work is involved, what is the rate of heat transfer to the water (in kW)?

A) 126 kW  
B) 252 kW  
C) 335 kW  
D) 670 kW

B

**2.** A rigid container with a volume of 0.5 m³ initially contains nitrogen gas at 200 kPa and 25°C. The gas is heated until its pressure reaches 400 kPa. Assuming constant specific heats (Cv = 0.744 kJ/kg·K), what is the final temperature? (Do NOT assume ideal gas behavior.)

A) 323 K  
B) 373 K  
C) 423 K  
D) 473 K

**3.** An open feedwater heater receives 10 kg/s of liquid water at 50°C (h = 209 kJ/kg) and mixes it with 5 kg/s of steam at 500 kPa, 200°C (h = 2870 kJ/kg). (Note: A negative value indicates heat removal.)

**6.** A throttling valve reduces the pressure of saturated steam from 1000 kPa to 100 kPa. What is the quality (dryness fraction) of the steam exiting the valve at 100 kPa? (Assume enthalpy remains constant across the valve.)

From NCEES tables:
- At 1000 kPa, saturated: h_f = 417.36 kJ/kg, h_fg = 2088.7 kJ/kg
- At 100 kPa, saturated: h_f = 417.36 kJ/kg, h_fg = 2257.9 kJ/kg

A) 0.45  
B) 0.62  
C) 0.78  
D) 0.92

**7.** In an ideal Rankine cycle (steam power plant), steam enters the turbine as saturated vapor at 5 MPa and exits as a two-phase mixture at 10 kPa with quality x = 0.82. If the mass flow rate is 15 kg/s, what is the turbine power output (in MW)?

From NCEES tables:
- At 5 MPa, saturated vapor: h_g = 2794.0 kJ/kg
- At 10 kPa, with x = 0.82: h = h_f + x·h_fg = 191.81 + 0.82(2392.8) = 2153 kJ/kg

A) 9.6 MW  
B) 14.4 MW  
C) 19.2 MW  
D) 28.8 MW
