## Tips for Using This Tracker

1. **Fill in daily.** Don't wait until end of week; write down time/progress immediately after study session.
2. **Be honest about time.** If you only studied 30 min, write 0.5 hours. No cheating yourself.
3. **Track score trends.** Plot your practice exam scores on a simple graph to see improvement trajectory.
4. **Adjust weekly.** If you're behind, increase daily study by 30 min. If ahead, keep the pace (don't coast).
5. **Review weaknesses.** At end of each week, identify topics that still need work and allocate time in next week.
6. **Celebrate progress.** If your practice exam score goes up, acknowledge it. You're doing the work.
