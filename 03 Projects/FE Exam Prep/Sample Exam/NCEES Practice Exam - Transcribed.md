**Q5.** A random variable $x$ has the probability distribution function $f(x)$ shown (a symmetric "M"/double-triangle shape over roughly −1 to 1). You obtain five samples and calculate the mean $\bar{x}$. You repeat the sampling process $k$ times ($k$ very large) and then plot a histogram for $\bar{x}$. The histogram of $\bar{x}_k$ is expected to look most like:
- **Correct: a normal (bell-shaped) distribution** — by the Central Limit Theorem, the distribution of sample means approaches a normal distribution regardless of the underlying $f(x)$.

**Q37.** Two vessels are being considered for use as a blending tank.
