## 🎬 Content Types to Try

### High-Engagement Posts (Aim for 50%+)
- **Speedpaints** — Time-lapse of art creation (1-3 min)
- **Before/After** — Show progression or sketch to final
- **Process breakdown** — Step-by-step walkthrough
- **Art trends** — Participate in trending audio/challenges

### Medium-Engagement Posts (Aim for 30%)
- **Finished pieces** — Your best work, high quality
- **WIP (Work-in-Progress)** — Show current project
- **Tutorial/Tips** — Quick art advice or technique
- **Q&A responses** — Answer audience questions

### Personality/Growth Posts (Aim for 20%)
- **Behind-the-scenes** — You creating, studio setup, talking
- **Personal story** — Why you make art, your journey
- **Mistakes/failures** — Relatable, humanizing
- **Appreciation posts** — Thank followers, celebrate milestones
