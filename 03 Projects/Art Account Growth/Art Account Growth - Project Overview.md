## 📱 Platform Strategy

### TikTok (Primary)
- **Posting cadence:** 2-3x per week (short-form, behind-the-scenes, tutorials, speedpaints)
- **Best times:** Post Mon-Wed, Thu-Sat (test to find audience)
- **Content mix:** 60% art process, 20% finished pieces, 20% personality/tips
- **Hashtags:** #DigitalArt, #ArtTok, #ArtProcess, [niche tags based on style]
- **Goal:** 500 followers by Sept 30

### Instagram (Future)
- **Posting cadence:** 1-2x per week (polished finished pieces)
- **Content:** Gallery posts + Reels (same content as TikTok, optimized)
- **Goal:** Launch by Oct 2026, reach 200 followers by end of year

### Other
- **Discord/Community:** Start small private Discord for supporters
- **Website/Portfolio:** Link in bio → Carrd or simple landing page (direct to commissions/Patreon)

## 💰 Monetization Pathways

### Immediate (Q3)
- [ ] **Commissions:** Set rates, create intake form
  - Simple portrait: $15-30
  - Complex character: $50-100
  - Full illustration: $75-150
  - Negotiate rates as demand increases

### Q4-2027
- [ ] **Patreon/Ko-fi:** $3, $8, $15 tier structure
  - Tier 1: Early access, WIP sneak peeks
  - Tier 2: Monthly requests, discount on commissions
  - Tier 3: Exclusive content, personal coaching

- [ ] **Digital Products:** 
  - Procreate brushes ($5-15)
  - Art templates ($3-8)
  - Tutorial videos ($10-30)

- [ ] **Etsy/Printful:**
  - Print-on-demand art prints
  - Stickers, mugs, apparel
  - Low effort once design is done

## 📈 Success Metrics

**Engagement:**
- Target 3-5% engagement rate (likes + comments / followers)
- Track which content types perform best
- Adjust based on data

**Growth:**
- Track follower growth daily/weekly
- Note when followers drop (content issue or just algorithm)
- Celebrate milestones: 100 ✅, 250, 500, 1k

**Revenue:**
- Track commission requests
- Monitor conversion rates (inquiries → actual commissions)
- Calculate revenue per hour
- Reinvest 20-30% back into tools/marketing
