## Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **2-month runway is tight** | May miss Sept deadline | Start sourcing immediately; cast wide net (USA + international); use recruiters for speed |
| **Visa sponsorship delays international opportunities** | Fewer options abroad | Prioritize US markets first; research visa timelines per country; ask about sponsorship early |
| **Procrastination/overthinking** | Momentum stalls | Weekly check-ins; clear action items each week; accountability from Claude |
| **Art business takes time away from job prep** | Divided focus | Keep TikTok/portfolio to 1–2 hours/week during search; scale post-hire |
| **$6k savings is tight for relocation** | Limited financial buffer | Prefer Denver/Seattle if relocation needed (lower cost); negotiate signing bonus/relocation package |
